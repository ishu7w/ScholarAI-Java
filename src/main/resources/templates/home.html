Questions, answered plainly

How is the AI score calculated? +

A published weighted model: Academic Performance 22%, Financial Need 18%, Achievements 12%, Research 10%, Leadership 8%, Projects & Skills 8%, SOP 8%, Community Service 7%, Recommendations 4%, Behaviour 3%. Every sub-score shows the exact inputs used — you can audit your own number.

Is demographic data used against applicants? +

Never. Gender, community, and disability fields are used exclusively for inclusion preferences on scholarships that explicitly target those groups (e.g. women-in-STEM). They are never used as penalties, and the fairness note on every match documents this.

How does document verification work? +

Text-based PDFs are checked against your profile. Differences are flagged for review. Images and scanned PDFs remain pending. A text match does not establish document authenticity.

Where do I apply, and how are decisions tracked? +

Apply now opens the provider’s official application page. For institution-managed submissions recorded here, the institution records a decision and reason in your workspace. External providers manage their own application status.

Can institutions customize the scoring weights? +

Institutions define eligibility criteria for each scholarship. The ten scoring weights are fixed and published so every ranking remains reproducible.
