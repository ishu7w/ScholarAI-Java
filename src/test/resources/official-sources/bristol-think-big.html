Think Big Scholarships for international students

Apply now for a Think Big Scholarship!

Think Big Scholarship applications for September 2027 entry are now open.

The application deadline is 19 March 2027 at 1 pm UK time.

Think Big is the University of Bristol’s flagship scholarship scheme, providing funding towards tuition fees for international students studying eligible undergraduate or postgraduate courses.

Eligibility

You can apply if you: 

  • have already applied to start an eligible course in September 2027
  • and you are classed as an overseas student for fee purposes. 

You do not need to have received an offer to study before submitting an international scholarship application. 

Eligible courses 

  • Any undergraduate course for up to four years of study delivered in person. 
  • Any one-year postgraduate taught or master’s by research programme delivered in person. 

Non-eligible courses

Distance learning, online and part-time courses and programmes are not eligible.

How to apply

Apply using the international scholarships online application form. 

By completing the application form, you will be considered for the following scholarships for which you are eligible: 

  • Think Big Scholarships 
  • GREAT Scholarships 
  • Mr. and Mrs. M S Siriwardena Memorial Scholarship
  • Michael Wong Pakshong bursary 

The terms and conditions are also the same for all listed scholarships. 

You can find more details about this in our guidance on how to apply for international scholarships. 

Deadline

Applications close at 1 pm (UK time) on Friday 19 March 2027. 

You will be contacted about the outcome of your application by the end of May 2027. 

Contact us

For scholarship question about these scholarships, contact international-scholarships@bristol.ac.uk.
